This is the first `workflow` audit; the 2026-09-17 `inventory.md` covers `docs` only, so there are no `offload` or `keep (revisit)` rows to carry forward.

A row is a passage: a line, or a tightly bound run of lines making one claim.
`keep` rows are listed only where the passage looked cuttable; a section with no row is `keep` throughout.
Line numbers are from the pre-prune tree and locate the passage; they are not carried into the prune.
`Passages` in the summary counts the prose lines outside frontmatter, fences, and headings; `keep` is the remainder after the applied verdicts.

## Rules applied

- **`(Refs #N)` on a workflow rule.**
  No ref in this corpus marks a lint-guarded boundary, an ADR, or a structural invariant; every one is provenance and is dropped (`compress`).
  The same holds for the phase-number precedents in `plan-improvements.md` and `finish-phase.md` ("Phase 21 planning silently dropped one").
- **`Refs #N` as syntax** (`pr-review.md`'s "reference the PR as `Refs #$1`", `finish-phase.md`'s "reference issues as `Refs #A, #Z`") and **issue numbers as data** (the `triage-backlog.md` sample rows, the `ship.md` step-heading example `([#885], with [#896])`, the `audit-agent-docs.md` examples) are kept.
- **Duplicate of a loaded skill.**
  A template passage is `delete` only when the template loads the skill that says it — `markdown-conventions` for the heredoc and ordered-list rules, `tidy-first` for the assessor's placement and deferral rules, `releasing` for the commit-type release rules, `testing` and `delegation` for their planning rules.
  Where the template does not load it (`tdd-plan.md`, `pr-review.md`, `retro-note.md`, `build-plan.md`'s conditional load), the passage stays.
- **Duplicate within a file or of `AGENTS.md`** is `delete`: `AGENTS.md` is in every session, and a restated step is paid twice per invocation.
- **Recurrence heuristic.**
  Two `delete` candidates on Q1 alone were checked against the retros: `retro.md`'s "This retro is where all four grow" appears in none (delete); `plan-issue.md`'s `--comments` note last appears in `packages/pi-permission-system/docs/retro/0741-gate-redirect-hosted-nested-commands.md` as a caught near-miss (kept).
- **Recap sections** (`## Constraints` in `ship.md`, `sync-worktree.md`, `ship-no-issue.md`) restate numbered steps; removing one is a heading removal, so each is `keep (revisit)`.
